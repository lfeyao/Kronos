# Research documentation

- Save trading research and team-facing experiment notes in this repository's `research/` directory, with topic subdirectories as useful.
- Update `research/README.md` when adding a research topic or deliverable.
- Record observation dates, data provenance, strategy parameters, costs, evaluation splits, results and limitations. Distinguish model-weight training from strategy parameter tuning.
- Preserve negative findings and identify reused evaluation periods. Do not claim exploratory results establish a trading edge.
- Include scripts and non-sensitive inputs needed to reproduce calculations when available. Never include API keys, private keys, access tokens or other credentials.
